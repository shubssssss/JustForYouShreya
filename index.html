Shreya, ❤️

Shreya,

I don't know how to start this. I'm not someone who writes stuff like this, so bear with me.

You remember that party? Room was loud, I was way too drunk, and somehow you ended up right next to me. I held your hand, don't even know why, it just happened. But that moment stuck with me. Before that night I hadn't even thought about you like this.

Since then I can't really explain it, you just started mattering to me. A lot. Honestly, you're what I'd call my dream girl — not just how you look, but everything about you. Your voice, the way you carry yourself, all of it.

You know I never text you. I always tell myself I'm just bad at texting. But truth is, I knew you didn't like texting or calls much, and that stuck in my head every time. I could've texted anyway but I was scared. Scared you'd get annoyed and stop talking to me. And honestly, a big part was your bf too — never had the guts to text or call because I didn't want him seeing it and you having to deal with that.

I know I shouldn't sit next to you as much as I do lol. But I keep finding some reason to anyway, it just makes me happy even if I never say why.

I've liked a few girls before, been in relationships too. But whatever this is, I haven't found it in anyone else.

I'm not good at expressing this stuff. Maybe I never will be. But I didn't want to keep pretending I don't feel it.

And now you're leaving. Not just this room, for good. Didn't really hit me until I sat down to write this. Feels weird thinking you won't be around anymore. Notice it more than I'd like to admit.

I know we can never really date, age gap and everything, I've made peace with that. But just remember, whenever you need anything, one call or text and I'll be there. Doesn't matter where I am. Even if we end up distant, even if you never wanna see my face again, I'll still come if you need me. Just remember that.

You'll probably think I'm an idiot for going this far when I could've just said it in a few lines. But saying stuff out loud has never worked for me. So this is what I could do instead.

Hope you like it. And don't worry, I'm not telling anyone about this. This link is only between you and me. Whenever I think of you I'll probably come back and add more to it.
